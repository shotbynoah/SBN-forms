Elopement Photography Agreement

Prepared for Chennel Johnson & Kevin  |  Event Date: July 31, 2026

This Elopement Photography Agreement ("Agreement") is made by and between SBN Media LLC, represented by Noah Kissinger ("Photographer"), and Chennel Johnson and Kevin (collectively, the "Clients"), regarding photography services for the Clients' elopement described below.

1. Event Details

Event Date: July 31, 2026

Location: 2040 Ferndale Rd., Arroyo Grande, California

Coverage Start Time: 4:30 PM

Total Coverage: 2 hours

2. Services & Deliverables

Photographer agrees to provide the following for the Event:

  • Two (2) hours of continuous on-site photography coverage of the elopement.
  • 48-Hour Sneak Peek — a curated set of edited preview images delivered within forty-eight (48) hours of the Event.
  • Final Gallery — a minimum of one hundred twenty (120) professionally edited, high-resolution digital images.
  • Delivery of the Final Gallery via a private, high-quality online digital album with download access, within   weeks of the Event date.

3. Investment & Payment

Total Package Investment: $650

Retainer (50%, non-refundable): $325 — due upon signing of this Agreement

Remaining Balance: $325 — due July 31, 2026 (day of the Event, prior to coverage start)

Accepted Payment Methods:  

The retainer is non-refundable and reserves the Event date exclusively for the Clients. The remaining balance is due in full on the day of the Event prior to the start of coverage. Failure to remit payment may result in pause or cancellation of services at Photographer's discretion.

4. Travel

Travel within Arroyo Grande, California is included in the package investment described above.

5. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • The retainer is non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • Clients may reschedule the Event date one time at no additional charge, subject to Photographer's availability, with written notice no later than fourteen (14) days before the original Event date.
  • If Photographer is unable to perform due to illness, emergency, or other circumstances beyond their control, Photographer will make a commercially reasonable effort to secure a comparable replacement photographer or will refund all monies paid.

6. Image Usage & Copyright

Photographer retains copyright of all images. Clients are granted a personal-use license to print, share, and reproduce delivered images for personal, non-commercial purposes. Photographer may use images from the Event for portfolio, marketing, website, and social media purposes unless Clients opt out in writing prior to the Event.

7. Limitation of Liability

In the unlikely event Photographer is unable to deliver images due to equipment failure, loss, theft, or file damage, Photographer's liability is limited to a full refund of monies paid under this Agreement. Photographer is not responsible for circumstances beyond reasonable control — including venue restrictions, weather, or third-party interference — that limit the ability to capture certain images.

8. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to causes beyond their reasonable control, including acts of God, natural disasters, or government restrictions. In such cases, the parties will work together in good faith to reschedule the Event.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements, whether written or oral. Any modification must be made in writing and signed by both parties.
